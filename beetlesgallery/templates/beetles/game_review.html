How labels become expert-backed

A player is an expert at a rank within a branch (species within a genus, genus within a tribe, tribe within a subfamily, or subfamily overall) after at least {{ needed }} validated examples there with a 95% Wilson lower bound on accuracy of at least 90%. A proposal is expert-backed at a rank when an expert for that exact branch agrees and no expert disagrees, and every rank above it is expert-backed too. Branches with too few validated ROIs to ever test instead accept players proven in two or more related branches (e.g. other genera of the same tribe). Accept or dismiss proposals on the annotation page.
