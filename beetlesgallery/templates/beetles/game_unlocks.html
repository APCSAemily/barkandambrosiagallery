What happens to your labels

{% if info.proposals %}

{% else %}

From level {{ proposal_level }} (with enough reliability), your names for beetles nobody has checked yet are sent to the curators as suggestions. Until then they help the game learn which beetles are hard.

{% endif %}

Only proven experts have their labels written into the database without a curator checking first. The bar is high:

  • You must prove yourself on beetles experts have already checked, in that exact group: at least {{ trust_min_judged }} answers there, and so accurate that we are 95% sure you are right at least {% widthratio trust_bound 1 100 %}% of the time.
  • You must also be among the most reliable players overall.
  • Being good in neighbouring groups is not enough: it lets your labels reach curators as suggestions, but not skip review.
  • At least {{ min_experts }} such experts must give the same species, and no proven player may disagree.

Expertise is earned group by group (subfamily, tribe, genus, species within a genus): someone can be an expert on Xyleborus and a beginner on Ips, so their labels may go straight in for one group and not for another. It is checked again all the time, so it can be lost. See your expertise tree.
